PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your road. Your data.

Your saved stops, notes, and trip live in this browser’s local storage. Your truck and trip setup also stay on this device. GPS starts only when enabled. Beta 2 sends selected route coordinates to OSRM when calculating routes; a GPS origin sends that start point. Raw live fixes stay in memory. Saved plans include their chosen start point. The app makes no background network requests and requires no account. Clearing browser data removes local changes, so export a backup when needed.

About this test catalog

“Operator listed” means an operator source supports the place identity. It is not an on-site visit or a current opening check. Blank amenities are unknown. Public facilities and unconfirmed listings carry visible review flags. Historical and excluded sites are hidden by default.
